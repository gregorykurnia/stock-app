The prior hash mismatch was reproduced: Next.js 16.2.10/Turbopack's compiled JSON module changed VOO history bar 278 from `180.93528747558594` to `180.9352874755859`. The page now reads raw JSON text and parses it on the Node server before checking the v2 canonical manifest and recalculating scores. This preserves the exact input without loosening comparison.
- An independent reference agreed across all 80 fund/cutoff/horizon rows within `1.4210854715202004e-14`, under a `1e-10` absolute tolerance. This validates arithmetic on the retained artifact, not Yahoo's historical adjustment quality or the scoring method's predictive value.
- The 37 declared one-factor scenarios cover normalized fee/history weights, individual history-component weights, fee scale, growth anchor/scale, and drawdown/downside scales. Across 2,960 scenario rows scores remain bounded; 15 of 50 mandate-matched comparison rows change order under at least one scenario, and 23 of 50 baseline comparisons tie when rounded to one decimal. Keep the method experimental and display at most one decimal, with no rank claims.
- Growth correlates with downside deviation at -0.900 in the 80 retained rows; drawdown magnitude correlates with downside deviation at 0.823. Treat those risk components as overlapping evidence, not independent signals.
- Eight common-cutoff fee rows are eligible using their recorded current disclosures. All 32 historical-cutoff fee rows remain blocked for point-in-time use. Found prospectuses are evidence leads only until availability, applicability, net/gross designation, supplements and waiver/expiry terms are resolved. Current fees held constant in 2019–2024 windows remain sensitivity analysis.
- The sample supports only the listed peer groups: VOO/IVV, VTI/ITOT/SCHB, and VXUS/IXUS. Index construction differs within the latter two groups. VEA has no peer in this sample. Do not pool mandates or create a universal rank.

This ID already covers the retained batch and two-fund trial; M3 must keep using it only while every parameter below remains unchanged. This decision does not approve the method for `/etf`, freeze it for release, or establish predictive value. M3 may return the candidate to M2 if its predeclared checks fail.

The M1 audit found no evidence that justifies changing parameters. Sensitivity results instead show unstable ordering (15/50 peer comparisons change order) and many one-decimal ties (23/50). The M1 independent calculation and integrity gates pass, so those findings support a carefully limited out-of-sample calculation check, not a release. The overlap between drawdown and downside deviation (correlation 0.823) remains an explicit interpretation limitation.

## Candidate configuration

Carry forward the existing formula unchanged for the next bounded validation: `(0.30 × fee points + 0.40 × historical outcome points) ÷ 0.70`, with fee scale 0.50%, growth anchor/scale 8%/4%, drawdown scale 25%, downside scale 15%, and historical component weights 0.40/0.35/0.25. This is a test candidate, not a frozen or released methodology. Any future evidence-backed change requires a new version and rerun of affected M1 checks.

The exact component formulas are `fee points = 100 × exp(−net expense ratio percent / 0.50)`, `growth points = 100 × logistic((CAGR percent − 8) / 4)`, `drawdown points = 100 / (1 + (drawdown magnitude percent / 25)²)`, and `downside points = 100 / (1 + (downside deviation percent / 15)²)`. CAGR compounds the exact monthly total returns and annualizes over actual endpoint days using 365.2425 days per year. Maximum drawdown is measured from the daily adjusted-close path. Downside deviation is `100 × sqrt(12 × Σ min(monthly return, 0)² / n)`. Historical outcome points are the weighted geometric mean of growth, drawdown and downside points at weights 0.40/0.35/0.25. Final points are `(3/7 × fee points) + (4/7 × historical outcome points)`. No weight is redistributed when an input is missing.

Limit eligibility to verified passive broad equity-index mandates with official issuer identity/mandate evidence, a dated official net expense ratio, and complete Yahoo adjusted-close history in USD. Identity, mandate, and fee evidence must be HTTPS documents on a verified issuer domain. M3 must add issuer domains through an explicit source review; an arbitrary HTTPS URL is not sufficient. A fee must be nonnegative, designated net, have a valid financial date, be no more than 365 calendar days old at capture, and have no expired or invalid waiver. Missing or gross-only fees are blockers; never infer or impute them.

At capture, Yahoo history must identify the adjusted-close endpoint and the ticker-specific Yahoo history URL, be denominated in USD, have a valid retrieval time no more than five calendar days old, and include a latest observed session no more than five calendar days old. Prices must be positive, dates valid and ordered, with no duplicates or future observations. Preserve the source's adjustment convention: distributions and splits are reflected in adjusted closes and must not be added again. Capture-time validity and current freshness are separate fields; a stale source blocks current availability but does not rewrite whether a retained capture passed when it was made.

Use one predeclared month-end U.S.-listed equity trading cutoff across the sample: the final completed U.S. equity session of a calendar month. A 1Y result requires exactly 12 consecutive month-end returns from 13 endpoints; a 3Y result requires exactly 36 from 37 endpoints. Both require all expected daily U.S. equity sessions from the first endpoint through cutoff, a valid cutoff, and the matching labeled horizon. Do not silently substitute a shorter history. Preserve `2026-09-30` and the four historical cutoffs in M1 as the fixed baseline set. M3's primary cross-section uses a common current market cutoff and current dated fee inputs; label market cutoff, fee date, and capture time separately. Historical-window results are point-in-time scores only when the fee document was published by that cutoff and shown to apply then, including net/gross and waiver/expiry terms. Otherwise they are market-window sensitivity only and must not be represented as historical scores. Never carry current fees backward or infer a missing fee.

Require integrity version `equity-index-integrity-v2`, serialization `canonical-json-key-order-v1`, and SHA-256 hashes over the retained artifact and metadata, fund profiles and full histories/provenance, every row's window inputs and result, and the scoring parameters. Preserve array order and exact parsed numeric values. On the Next.js page, read retained JSON as raw text in the Node runtime before parsing and replaying it; do not import the audited numeric artifact as a compiled JSON module. Recompute before display and block modified, missing, incomplete or mismatched inputs with precise reasons. A digest detects changes but is not a signature or proof of source authenticity.

Comparison eligibility requires the same candidate version, horizon, market cutoff and verified exposure/peer group. Keep the M1 groups VOO/IVV; VTI/ITOT/SCHB; and VXUS/IXUS, with their benchmark/index differences visible. Keep VEA unranked until a sourced developed-market peer is established. M3 must define any new groups from official mandate evidence before examining scores. Do not pool exposures or claim a universal rank. Display no more than one decimal place; a rounded tie does not mean identical returns or mandates. Retain component points and weights, raw outcomes, source links and dates, cutoff, capture-time validity, current freshness, run ID and blockers with each result.

Keep the label **Experimental · Yahoo adjusted-close equity-index candidate** wherever this method is shown. It is separate from the existing Core, Full Grand Score, cost-only and execution scorecards. No score bands, quality grades, winner claims, or prospective-return interpretation are supported by M1.

## M2 acceptance criteria and disposition

1. A reviewed candidate specification records version, formula, parameters, eligible mandates, mandatory source contracts, fee applicability policy, date/freshness rules, windows, integrity/hash schema, peer sets, precision, and excluded interpretations.
2. Every proposed difference from the captured baseline cites an M1 finding and is versioned; no parameter is changed to improve a selected fund's rank.
3. Historical point-in-time scoring remains unavailable for all 32 unresolved rows. Current-fee sensitivity remains visibly distinct from historical-fee scoring.
4. The exact Next.js numeric-precision regression and raw-text parsing fix are documented.
5. M1 mutation tests cover altered source, history, fee, parameters, saved score, incomplete rows and canonical numeric precision; the production build rendered the passing artifact and blocked a precision-altered artifact. Evidence is recorded in the dated audit.
6. The candidate is declared ready for M3's previously unused sample under the fixed protocol in the [M3 handoff](etf-scoring-m3-handoff.md). No M3 sample acquisition or scoring is part of M2.

**Disposition: pass to M3 validation only.** All M2 specification items are recorded above. There are no parameter changes from M1, so no formula retune or new M1 replay is needed. The historical fee branch remains blocked for all 32 historical rows. The evidence, limitations, and validation checks needed for M3 are fixed in the linked handoff. This is not a release or a production methodology approval.
